Commentary
Macro Backdrop & Market Narrative
The primary narrative this week was the re-escalation of Middle East geopolitical risk, which caught risk assets off guard as markets opened Monday. The tail risk of a sustained Iran conflict — while not our base case — is materially higher than at any point in 2024 or 2025, and has raised the probability of a $100/bbl Brent scenario that warrants tactical caution before buying near-term dips.

Against this backdrop, the 'Goldilocks' macro environment remains broadly intact. US PPI came in firmer than expected but our estimates are unchanged; initial claims held steady; and consumer confidence improved in February alongside re-accelerating house prices. We remain well below consensus on February nonfarm payrolls (25k vs. 70k), which if realised, would reignite rate cut pricing and provide support to duration-sensitive names in the portfolio.

In Europe, high-frequency indicators point to resilient euro area momentum. Bunds are enjoying their strongest start to the year since 2021 — we turn short German rates here, targeting a return to 2.85% on 10y as growth surprises to the upside. The UK's Green Party by-election win adds political noise but does not alter our macro view.
